ALDI Priano Protein Tortelloni recall: botulism risk after a cold-chain failure

FDABotulismALDI Published November 19, 2026

ALDI is recalling two refrigerated pastas sold under its Priano store brand: Priano Protein Carbonara Tortelloni and Priano Protein Herb & Cheese Tortelloni. According to the notice published by the FDA on November 17, 2026, the products may not have been kept at proper temperatures, which could allow Clostridium botulinum, the bacterium behind botulism, to grow.

Which ALDI tortelloni are recalled

  • Priano Protein Carbonara Tortelloni, 9 oz plastic package;
  • Priano Protein Herb & Cheese Tortelloni, 9 oz plastic package;
  • both with lot number T9101926 and a best-if-used-by date of December 18, 2026.

They were sold at ALDI stores in Alabama, Georgia, North Carolina and South Carolina, in store and online. Only this lot is listed: Priano tortelloni with another lot number or date are not part of this recall.

The best-by date is about a month away, so packages bought in recent weeks may still be in the refrigerator.

Why a temperature problem means a botulism risk

The recall is not based on a positive test: the notice says the products may not have been stored, distributed or sold at the appropriate temperatures. For refrigerated, sealed foods, that is enough to act. Clostridium botulinum grows without oxygen and produces a toxin; keeping these products cold is one of the main barriers against it.

Botulinum toxin cannot be seen, smelled or tasted. That is why the FDA warns consumers not to prepare or eat the recalled tortelloni even if the package looks normal, and not to taste the product to check it.

It is the second large recall this fall caused by temperature control rather than contamination found in a lab: Kayem recalled about 1.58 million pounds of hot dogs and deli meats in October after a warehouse cooler ran warm.

Botulism: the symptoms to know

No illnesses had been reported as of the recall notice. Botulism is rare but serious. According to the CDC, symptoms start in the head and move down the body: double or blurred vision, drooping eyelids, slurred speech, difficulty swallowing, then muscle weakness that can spread and affect breathing. They usually appear 18 to 36 hours after eating contaminated food, and occasionally days later.

Botulism is a medical emergency: anyone with these symptoms should get care immediately, and mention what they ate.

What to do

Check your refrigerator for Priano Protein Carbonara Tortelloni or Priano Protein Herb & Cheese Tortelloni, 9 oz, lot T9101926, best by 12/18/2026. Do not cook, eat or taste them, even if the package looks fine. Throw them away or return them to any ALDI store for a full refund. If you or someone in your home has symptoms of botulism after eating them, call 911 or go to the emergency room.
