Breakfast biscuit recall: Listeria in vending-machine sandwiches and gravy across 9 states, traced to one biscuit supplier

FDAUSDAListeria Published November 23, 2026

A recall of breakfast foods for possible Listeria monocytogenes keeps growing. At its source are biscuits recalled by C.H. Guenther & Son, used as an ingredient by other companies. The latest expansion, published by the FDA on November 18, 2026, adds a sausage, egg & cheese biscuit sandwich and a biscuit & sausage gravy sold in seven Southern states, mostly through vending machines and break rooms.

The breakfast products recalled so far

Both come from Fresh & Ready Foods LLC, made at its Longview, Texas facility and distributed from October 15 to November 11, 2026:

  • Fresh To You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sandwich, 5.7 oz, clear plastic film, SKU 3100048, "Fresh Thru" dates 11/12 to 11/22 — sold in Alabama, Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Oklahoma, Tennessee and Texas;
  • Mmm! Meals Made in Minutes Biscuit & Sausage Gravy, 9.5 oz, clear clamshell, SKU 90013, "EXP" dates 11/12 to 11/18 — sold in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Oklahoma, Tennessee and Texas.

An earlier recall, on November 8, covered 2,414 Fresh To You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sandwiches (4.6 oz, UPC 8 405493 0168 4, "Fresh Thru" 11/07 to 11/19) made at the company's Renton, Washington facility and sold in Oregon and Washington from October 25 to November 4. That brings the total to nine states.

The dates have passed, but the USDA notes that some product may still be in refrigerators or freezers.

One ingredient, two agencies

The biscuits themselves are regulated by the FDA; a sandwich or a gravy that contains sausage is regulated by the USDA. That is why this recall shows up in two places. On November 14, the USDA's Food Safety and Inspection Service issued a public health alert for meat and poultry products containing the recalled biscuits, updated on November 19 to add products. The FSIS warns that more products may be identified as the biscuit recall moves through the supply chain.

This is the pattern of an ingredient recall: one supplier's batch reaches several manufacturers, each of which then announces its own recall, under its own brand, days apart. A shopper who heard about "the biscuit recall" has no way to know which finished products contain those biscuits.

Bought from a vending machine: hard to trace

These breakfasts were sold through employee break rooms, grab-and-go coolers, refrigerated vending machines and micro-markets. There is no receipt and no store loyalty account to notify buyers. The only way to check is the package itself: the brand, the SKU and the date printed on the front label.

Listeria: who is at risk

No illnesses have been reported. According to the FSIS, listeriosis mainly affects older adults, people with weakened immune systems, and pregnant women and their newborns. It can cause fever, muscle aches, headache, stiff neck, confusion and loss of balance; in pregnancy it can lead to miscarriage or stillbirth. People in these groups who develop flu-like symptoms within two months of eating a recalled product should see a doctor and mention it.

What to do

Do not eat Fresh To You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sandwiches (SKU 3100048) or Mmm! Meals Made in Minutes Biscuit & Sausage Gravy (SKU 90013) with the dates above, including any kept in a freezer. Throw them away. For a refund or questions: RecallConcern@HotLineGlobal.com or 1-855-424-8390, weekdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. ET. USDA Meat and Poultry Hotline: 888-674-6854.
