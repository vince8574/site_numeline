Costco Kirkland Signature whipped cream recall: cleaning chemical, and how to read the code on your can

CostcoChemicalKirkland Signature Published November 19, 2026

Alamance Foods is recalling Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream, sold at Costco in 12 states, because one batch may contain a small amount of a cleaning chemical. According to the letter sent to Costco members on November 7, 2026, members reported a chemical smell and taste. No serious adverse effects requiring medical care have been reported.

Which whipped cream is recalled

  • Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream, 15 oz (425 g) aerosol can, sold in 3-packs — Costco item #1777000, item code AC50;
  • best-if-used-by date APR/02/27;
  • only cans with a production time stamp between 00:35 and 02:45;
  • bought between October 11 and October 31, 2026.

It was sold at Costco in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Mississippi, Missouri, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee and Wisconsin.

How to check your can

The code is printed in black directly on the red can, on two lines (the recall letter shows a photo of it): a production code with a time (hours:minutes), then BEST IF USED BY APR 02 27. A can is concerned only if the date is April 2, 2027 and the time is between 00:35 and 02:45. A can printed with "01:03", for example, is in the recalled range; one printed with "03:10" is not.

This is a useful detail: a single best-by date can cover a whole day of production, and the time stamp is what separates the batch with a problem from the rest. It is exactly the kind of check that is easy to get wrong by eye.

Not yet in the FDA records

The recall was announced by the manufacturer and Costco, which contacted members using its purchase records. As of November 19, 2026, it does not appear in the FDA's recall announcements or enforcement database. That is common: the FDA often classifies a recall days or weeks after the company starts it. We will update the current food recalls page when it does.

Timing matters here: whipped cream is a holiday staple, and Thanksgiving is on November 26. Cans bought in October have a best-by date months away and may be waiting in the refrigerator for the holidays.

What to do

Check your refrigerator for Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream, 15 oz, best by APR/02/27, with a time stamp between 00:35 and 02:45. Stop using it and return it to any Costco for a full refund. Questions: Alamance Foods, customer.care@alamancefood.com. If you notice a chemical smell or taste in any food, do not eat it; if someone feels unwell after eating it, contact a healthcare provider or Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).
