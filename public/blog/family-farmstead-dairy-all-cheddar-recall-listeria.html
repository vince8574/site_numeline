Family Farmstead Dairy recalls all its cheddar for Listeria: from one lot to every lot in four weeks

FDAListeriaRaw milk Published November 26, 2026

Family Farmstead Dairy, of Worcester, New York, is recalling all of its cheddar cheese because it may be contaminated with Listeria monocytogenes. The notice, published by the FDA on November 10, 2026, comes less than four weeks after the same dairy recalled a single lot of raw cheddar for E. coli.

Which Family Farmstead cheddar is recalled

  • Family Farmstead Raw Mild Cheddar, 8 oz, plastic wrapped;
  • Family Farmstead Raw Sharp Cheddar, 8 oz, plastic wrapped;
  • all lot codes dated 10/16/27 and earlier.

The cheese was sold in retail stores in 12 states: New York, New Jersey, Vermont, New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Connecticut, Delaware, Virginia, West Virginia and Tennessee. The notice gives no UPC: check the brand, the product name and the date printed on the label. The company says no other Family Farmstead products are included.

From one lot to every lot

This is the second recall from the dairy this fall, and it is much wider than the first:

RecallWhatWhy
October 15One lot of Raw Mild Cheddar (7/22/27), 7 statesE. coli O26:H11 and O45:H2
November 10All Raw Mild and Raw Sharp Cheddar dated 10/16/27 and earlier, 12 statesPossible Listeria monocytogenes

The October recall started from a sample tested in Maryland, as we reported in our article on the first recall. The November notice does not say how the Listeria risk was found, whether by a laboratory result, an inspection or the company's own checks. It says the decision "follows" the October recall, without explaining the link. No illnesses are mentioned.

Recalling every lot rather than one is what a producer does when it cannot rule out that a problem affects all of its production. A cutoff of 10/16/27 also means the recall reaches back over many months of cheese.

Why the dates matter

The best-by dates on these cheeses run about a year ahead: a block bought this summer can still be well within its date today. Raw-milk cheddar is made from milk that has not been pasteurized, so it does not go through the heating step that kills bacteria such as Listeria, which can also keep growing slowly in the refrigerator.

According to the CDC, listeriosis is most dangerous for pregnant women and their newborns, adults aged 65 and older, and people with weakened immune systems. Symptoms can appear weeks after eating contaminated food.

What to do

Check your refrigerator for Family Farmstead Raw Mild Cheddar or Raw Sharp Cheddar, 8 oz, with a date of 10/16/27 or earlier. Do not eat it; return it to the store, or contact Family Farmstead Dairy at (607) 397-4044 (weekdays, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.) or customerservice@familyfarmsteaddairy.com. Clean any surface, knife or container that touched it. If someone at higher risk develops fever or flu-like symptoms after eating it, contact a healthcare provider and mention the cheese.
