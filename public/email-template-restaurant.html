Every week, the FDA and USDA pull products off the market — contaminated meat, recalled dairy, mislabeled allergens. Most restaurant owners find out after a customer gets sick. By then, the damage is done: a health inspection, a social media firestorm, or worse.

Numeline changes that. Point your phone at any lot number or barcode on your packaging — the app cross-checks it against the official FDA and USDA recall databases and gives you an instant answer:

✓ SAFE — serve it

⚠ RECALLED — pull it now

Three things that set Numeline apart:

— 5-second checks. Scan any lot number or barcode, get an instant result. No more 30-minute manual lookups on the FDA website.

— Lot-level precision. We check your exact batch — not just the brand. So you know if your specific product is affected.

— Recall alerts. If a product you've scanned gets recalled later, you're notified immediately.
